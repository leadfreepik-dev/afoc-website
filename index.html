ADSS Cables

Self-supporting optical cable engineered for aerial distribution, campus loops and medium-to-long span routes.

APPLICATION

Aerial outside-plant networks, including deployment along existing rights of way and transmission towers.

CONSTRUCTION

Loose-tube fibers around an FRP central member, reinforced with high-strength aramid yarns and protected by an HDPE jacket.
