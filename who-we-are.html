Arabian Fiber Optic Cable Manufacturing LLC (AFOC) is a UAE-based manufacturer delivering reliable, performance-driven fiber optic cable solutions for telecommunications, data networking and large-scale infrastructure projects. AFOC combines advanced technology, strict quality standards and an application-focused approach to deliver tailored cable systems.
